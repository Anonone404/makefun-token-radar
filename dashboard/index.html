    How the model was tested

    The value model is fitted on the snapshot’s live tokens — and then tested on tokens it never saw. A median error of 3× is the honest level: launch values are hard to predict, and the numbers below say so.

    Every term tested

    Each row drops one term, refits the model from scratch, and re-runs both held-out tests. The numbers are the median miss with the term gone — compare them with the full model’s 3.32× (leave-one-out) and 3.00× (walk-forward). No term’s removal improves both tests, so every term stays.

    Walk-forward matters most: the fit trained on pre-2025 launches underpredicts 2025 launches, so the board’s implied values are probably low for the youngest protocols. Launch odds are base rates from the peers’ record, not forecasts.

    How the ranking works

    Every number above comes from one sealed snapshot of the protocol universe (—). The board ranks 290 protocols: every pre-token protocol that is not a wrapped, staked, bridged or exchange product, plus every live-token protocol at its market cap for comparison. A further 152 protocols record a token but no market cap, and 316 products are excluded as wrappers or exchange products — they are listed or explained, never silently dropped.

    log mcap = 10.57 + 0.36 × log TVL − 0.10 × growth − 0.12 × log age − 0.15 × log chains + category offset

    • Fitted, not assumed — a ridge regression (λ = 10, chosen by leave-one-out among 0, 1 and 10) on 92 live tokens, in the log of market cap. The growth term is shrunk hard: the raw fit’s −1.9 is noise, and the ridge keeps only −0.10. Value grows slower than TVL (a doubling of TVL adds 28%), and older protocols are cheaper per TVL dollar.
    • Category offsets — shrunk mean residuals for categories with at least 3 live tokens (Lending −0.72, Basis Trading +0.64, Privacy +0.56, Cross Chain Bridge +0.39, RWA +0.21, Dexs +0.05, Indexes −0.02, CDP −0.37, Yield −0.46, Yield Aggregator −0.44). A safety clamp keeps every implied value between 0.005× and 2× TVL; it binds on no row.
    • Launch odds — a life table over the snapshot’s own record. Of protocols tracked 3 years or more, 86% record a token; that is the assumed eventual rate. Still pre-token protocols carry 81% (under 6 months), 78% (6–12 months), 77% (1–2 years) or 63% (2–3 years) of that rate, times a category multiplier from mature protocols (Risk Curators 0.30, Lending 1.27, the rest near 1), floored at 5% and capped at 95%.
    • Verdict — farm, thin or skip from expected value per dollar: implied value × launch odds, divided by the farm’s cost (capital $50–$250 by TVL tier plus gas $25, or $80 on expensive chains). The board splits into exact thirds on that efficiency. The verdict compares rows with each other; it does not promise dollars. The farmer’s share of any launch is unknown — a skip can still be the biggest launch on the board, it just earns less per dollar than two-thirds of the board.

    The live-token tab shows what the market itself says those protocols are worth, for comparison: a live token's market cap is not a model's guess, and the detail view shows what the model would have priced it at. Farm plans are per protocol, with a cost and a time on every step.
