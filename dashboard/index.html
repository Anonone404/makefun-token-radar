How the ranking works

Every number below comes from one sealed snapshot of the protocol universe (—). A protocol is ranked only when the snapshot records no token for it — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol — and it is not a wrapped, staked, bridged or exchange product of a token that already trades. The board covers the whole watchlist plus every other protocol the same rule keeps, at any size.

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier × breadth multiplier

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap / TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category, read from this same snapshot (at least 3 comparables, else the snapshot-wide median), clamped to 0.05–0.60.
  • Growth multiplier — 1 + (7-day TVL change, clamped to −60…+120%) × 0.5.
  • Maturity multiplier — 0.6 + min(age, 3 years) / 3 years × 0.4; a protocol with no listing date counts as 1 year old.
  • Breadth multiplier — 1 + 0.02 per extra chain beyond the first, at most 1.20.

The live-token tab shows what the market itself says those protocols are worth, for comparison: a live token's market cap is not a model's guess. Farm plans are per protocol, with a cost and a time on every step.
